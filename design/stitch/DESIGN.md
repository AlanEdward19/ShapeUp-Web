## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an energized, premium fitness atmosphere designed around the "Warm Oxide" aesthetic. Rejecting the sterile, clinical neon or cold slate-blue archetypes of conventional workout tracking software, it roots itself in high-end studio warmth, earth-tempered endurance, and disciplined vitality. 

The aesthetic is built on:
- **Warm Grounding:** Dark surfaces steeped in deep charcoal and roasted umber undertones, providing an inviting, low-fatigue backdrop for both daytime training sessions and late-night reflection.
- **Organic Intensity:** Burnt terracotta primaries inject focused vitality and momentum without visual agitation, complemented by grounding sage and olive tones for recovery, metrics, and progress signals.
- **Architectural Craft:** Tactile surfaces, low-contrast warm boundaries, and organic glow elevations cultivate an understated, professional-grade athletic interface suitable for trainers, members, and high-performance operators alike.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around warm earthen chromas to avoid digital sterility and enhance readability under dark mode.

- **Primary (`#e06c43`):** Burnt terracotta. Drives key training triggers, primary active states, heart rate peaks, and core calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#7d9b68`):** Muted athletic olive. Represents active recovery, rest intervals, completed objectives, and healthy status tiers.
- **Tertiary (`#d4a359`):** Burnished amber. Used for secondary streaks, milestone achievements, and warning indicators.
- **Neutral (`#211a17`):** Roasted oxide base. Serves as the canvas floor (`#1a1412`), container surface tier (`#241d1a`), and elevated overlay plane (`#2f2521`).

### Surface Hierarchy & Supporting Hues
- **Text Primary:** Off-white warm cream (`#f5ede6`) across all core titles and vital data readouts.
- **Text Muted:** Calibrated warm clay (`#a89990`) for auxiliary labels, metadata, and passive units.
- **Surface Borders:** Warm hairline oxide (`#3b322e`) maintaining structured containment without stark contrast breaks.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between compressed, industrial urgency and readable, humanistic documentation:

- **Display & Headings (Barlow Condensed):** Delivers a high-density, athletic presence inspired by analog gym clocks, field scoreboards, and performance telemetry. Used strictly with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and natural case formatting (avoid forced full-uppercase in long runs) to maintain an elevated, editorial tone.
- **Body & Controls (Source Sans 3):** Provides an open, unhurried counterweight to the condensed headings. Optimized for high glanceability across rapid biometric metrics, workout logs, nutrition tables, and program descriptions.
- **Metric Figures:** Primary data callouts (e.g., Reps, Watts, BPM) leverage Barlow Condensed at bold weights to guarantee instant legibility during movement.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model prioritizes disciplined density balanced with generous breathing room between discrete workout modules and training blocks:

- **Layout Grid:** 12-column responsive fluid grid for desktop and tablet landscape views; 6-column grid for tablet portrait; 4-column fluid stack for mobile viewports.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Structured on an 8px baseline (`0.5rem`). Component interiors use tight increments (`space-xs` through `space-md`) to ensure dense, cohesive functional groupings, while card matrices and module boundaries employ `space-lg` to `space-xl` to prevent cognitive fatigue during intense sessions.
- **Form-Factor Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** Max layout container bounded at 1440px with `margin-lg` outer offsets to preserve focal tracking.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Dynamic sidebar retraction into a rail format; metrics cards compress into a 2x2 grid.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Navigation transitions to a tactile bottom dock; gutters ease down to `gutter-sm` to maximize horizontal real estate for exercise logs and data tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established using warm-shifted tonal stacking and soft, diffused under-glows rather than harsh, pure-black drop shadows:

- **Canvas Foundation:** Layer 0 sits at deep roasted charcoal (`#181311`).
- **Card and Tile Tier:** Layer 1 rests at `#221c19` outlined by a subtle perimeter line of `#3b322e` at 1px thickness.
- **Floating Controls & Modals:** Layer 2 elevates to `#2c231f` supported by an ambient oxide shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 12, 10, 0.7), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(224, 108, 67, 0.08)`.
- **Active Glow Cue:** High-priority items, active timers, and selected workout routines cast a faint, organic terracotta radial halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(224, 108, 67, 0.25)`.
- **Depth Boundaries:** Never use bright gray or white dividers. Rely on structural spacing or 1px strokes of `#3b322e` with inset highlights where structural separation is necessary.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, confident contour language that reflects athletic precision:

- **Base Cards and Panels:** Set at `0.875rem` (14px), striking an equilibrium between geometric structure and warm ergonomics.
- **Action Buttons & Interactive Chips:** Inherit the same subtle radius or scale up to pill-form (`9999px`) exclusively for lightweight metric tags and status toggles.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces:** Elements nested inside parent cards (e.g., metric bar tracks, exercise row containers) taper down to `0.5rem` (8px) to respect visual concentricity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with burnt terracotta (`#e06c43`), labeled in dark charcoal (`#181311`) bold type for maximum punchy contrast. Hover state brightens to `#e87a53` with a warm glow bloom (`0 4px 16px rgba(224, 108, 67, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface background (`#28201c`) paired with a 1px border of `#3b322e` and warm cream text (`#f5ede6`). Hover state shifts border to terracotta tint (`#e06c43`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Unfilled; displays warm clay text (`#a89990`) transitioning to `#f5ede6` with an active background glow of `rgba(224, 108, 67, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Metric Tiles
- Encased in Layer 1 surface (`#221c19`) with a continuous 1px `#3b322e` border.
- Headers integrate a condensed title (`Barlow Condensed`, 600 weight) with an optional auxiliary badge aligned top-right.
- Key biometrics and performance values within cards render large (`headline-lg`) in cream (`#f5ede6`), accompanied by compact unit labels (`label-sm`) in muted clay (`#a89990`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Compact geometry with 0.5rem or full pill radius. Completed states use olive tint background (`rgba(125, 155, 104, 0.15)`) with olive text (`#7d9b68`).
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Default to `#241d1a` background; selected states transition to primary terracotta fill with high-contrast dark text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature deep charcoal troughs (`#1c1614`) inset against Layer 1 cards with a 1px border of `#3b322e`.
- Placeholders sit at warm clay (`#a89990`). Focused state introduces an active 1.5px border of `#e06c43` and a 2px diffuse outer wash of `rgba(224, 108, 67, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base form has an oxide outline (`#3b322e`) on a recessed background.
- Active state snaps to `#e06c43` background with an off-white cream checkmark icon. Radio selections utilize a centered inner dot with `0 0 8px rgba(224, 108, 67, 0.4)` glow.

### Workout List Rows & Progression Bars
- **List Items:** Modular table or stacked cards separated by 1px bottom lines of `#2c231f`. Hovering prompts a subtle horizontal slide with an accent terracotta border on the leading left edge.
- **Progress Gauges:** Track backdrop in `#2c231f`; active fill uses a linear blend from burnt terracotta (`#e06c43`) to athletic olive (`#7d9b68`) for holistic completion tracking.
